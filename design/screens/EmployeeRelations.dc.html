Protections & appeals

  • No victimisation or retaliation for a grievance raised in good faith, or for taking part in one.
  • Grievances are dealt with promptly and confidentially.
  • Appeals in writing, on grounds of procedural unfairness or new / material evidence.
  • Code of Conduct breaches reported by staff (HR-02 §6) are logged here as grievances.

HR-03 says "within a reasonable timeframe" for appeals and "promptly" for grievances. The system needs a number of days for each. Please confirm with HOD HR.
