Internship rules · stipend rules not yet in a policy

  • Placed interns: direct manager is HOD HR (Angels), who approves leave and HR matters; the client supervisor is the functional manager. [Proposed]
  • Intern leave is not yet covered by HR-06: a rule is needed before interns use the leave portal. [Open]
  • Stipend is paid only when the month’s logbook and attendance are signed by the client supervisor. [Proposed]
  • Unauthorised absence is deducted pro rata from the stipend. [Proposed]
  • Client organisation confirms requirements and supervises; Angels runs recruitment, keeps records and holds safeguarding duty of care (HR-04-INT).
  • At completion: certificate issued, then absorbed into a vacant post or exited.
  • Outcome tracked 6 and 12 months after completion for funder reporting.
