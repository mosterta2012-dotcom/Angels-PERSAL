How performance is managed

  1. 1 Set KPAs 5–8 from the role KPA library, agreed and signed before the period
  2. 2 Ongoing feedback Supervision, timely and documented
  3. 3 Formal review HR-07 Review Form, scale 1–5
  4. 4 Informal support Discussion, coaching, training
  5. 5 PIP If no improvement · HR-07-FRM-01
  6. 6 Escalate Persistent or wilful → HR-03 discipline
