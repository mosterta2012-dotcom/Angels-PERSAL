Disciplinary case DC-26-005 · HR-03-PROC-01

  1. 1Done Matter reported Breach of HR-02 or misconduct
  2. 2Done Investigation HR-03-SOP-01: scope, evidence, interviews, findings, report
  3. 3Done Suspension? Only for serious misconduct, pending investigation
  4. 4Done Notice of allegations Employee informed in writing
  5. 5Now Hearing Response & mitigating factors · HR-03-FRM-01
  6. 6Next Written outcome Sanction filed confidentially by HR
  7. 7Next Appeal Procedural unfairness or new evidence

For minor matters steps 2–5 can be replaced by informal counselling or a verbal warning. Dismissal creates a 5.1 termination request (letter HR-05-LET-03).
