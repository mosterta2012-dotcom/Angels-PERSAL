Job description changes during the year

1. Annual increase first: the person moves on their current level and step.2. Extra duties at the same level: no regrade. Pay changes only through the next annual review.3. Job regraded to a higher level: a promotion. Placed on the lowest step of the new level that is at or above current pay, never lower.4. An increase already given for a JD change, outside a scale, is not taken back. At implementation the salary is placed with the calculator: between steps, above step 5 or below step 1.5. The annual increase and a regrade are not added together for the same change; the regrade date is recorded separately on the person’s history.
Request 4.3 · Regrade after a job description change: HOD → HR (grading) → HOD HR → Head of Finance → CEO.

Rules

1. Jobs are graded, not people: the level comes from the job description.2. New appointments start on step 1 unless the starting step guide allows higher.3. Annual review (HR-07): rating 3 → 1 step; ratings 4–5 → 2 steps; ratings 1–2 → no step and a performance plan.4. Step 5 is the maximum. After that only the annual cost-of-living increase applies.5. Anyone already paid above step 5 keeps their salary and gets smaller increases until the scale catches up.6. Anyone below step 1 is brought up in phases through the annual increase cycle.7. A move to a higher level is a promotion into an approved, funded post.8. One increase date for everyone ([start of the financial year]): the annual increase and the review step happen together. Off-cycle increases only for a regrade or promotion, each through its own request.9. Funder-paid posts follow the same scale; the Head of Finance checks the budget, and if a funder will not cover an increase the reason is recorded.10. Every increase is kept on the person’s history with its date, type (annual, regrade, promotion) and approver.
Outside the scale: mentors and other SLA contractors (rate bands, HR-09), interns (stipend scale, HR-04-INT-PROC-01), bursary holders (award, HR-08), volunteers (expenses only), and outsourced suppliers.
