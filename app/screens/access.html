Roles & access

Personnel records stay with four HR roles (POPIA / PAIA). Staff and managers only use the separate leave portal. Sign-in with Microsoft 365; every action is in the audit log.

Access rules

1. Only HOD HR, HR Administrator, Head of Finance and CEO log into PERSAL.2. The leave portal shows a person their own leave, and a manager only their direct reports’ requests.3. A direct manager must be an employee. Functional managers and contractors approve nothing.4. Nobody approves their own request; the Board steps in when the CEO is the subject.5. Staff ask for their own data through the Information Officer (POPIA / PAIA).5a. HR risks leave PERSAL only as risk details (ID, area, level, owner, dates, status) for the QMS risk tracker; never names or personal data.6. Every view of a pay or ID field, and every change, is written to the audit log.
