Entitlements · HR-06 Leave Policy

Leave typeEntitlementPaidRule built into PERSAL
Annual15 working days per year (1 day per 17 worked); 20 after 5 completed yearsYesCycle from the employment date. Taken in the Dec–Jan closure; other times only by exception. Carried-over leave must be used within 4 months of the new cycle (longer only with management approval); staff warned at 60 and 30 days. Applies to permanent, fixed-term and part-time employees. Public holidays are never counted as leave. Where legislation and policy conflict, legislation prevails.
