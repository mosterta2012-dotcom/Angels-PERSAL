How the tracker works

1. A new HR risk, a level change or a status change reaches you here and by email or Teams the same day. 2. You give it a QMS ID in the QMS risk register; both registers stay linked. 3. You are warned when a risk is not reported in time (Critical: immediately; High: [48 hours]; Medium: [5] working days), or when a review or action is overdue. 4. A risk shows Closed only after the owner’s closure note, an evidence reference and HOD HR approval (CEO too for High and Critical). 5. Closed risks stay on the quarterly report for one more quarter as “closed this quarter”. Quarterly report goes to [HOD Operations and Systems · to confirm].
