Establishment rules 1. People are appointed only to a funded, vacant post. No post, no appointment, no payroll instruction. 2. Posts are created or closed only through a request approved by the CEO. 3. Every post has one cost centre and one funder, so salary costs roll up to programme budgets automatically. 4. Senior appointments are approved by the Board (HR-04 §5.1). Rules 1–3 are in draft HR-05-PROC-01. 5. Every post has one direct manager, who must hold an employee post: they approve leave, reviews and discipline. A functional manager (any post, including a contractor) guides day-to-day work but approves nothing. 6. Levels L1–L6 (Paterson) are proposed in the grading discussion paper and not yet approved; each title has 5 salary steps (see Grading & salary scales). Contractors, interns and bursary holders sit outside the salary scale.
